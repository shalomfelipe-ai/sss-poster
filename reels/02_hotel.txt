Three a.m., knocking at my hotel door. A voice says it's the room service I ordered. 🛎️

I didn't order anything, and the peephole shows an empty hallway — but the voice just keeps explaining, patiently, that I forgot. In the morning there's a tray at the foot of my bed: a plate scraped clean, and two sets of used cutlery.
